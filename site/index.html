    For sellers

    Your stall, open anywhere, any time

    Clear out your wardrobe, run your small shop or sell your collectibles: ShaPop brings you live buyers and guaranteed payment.

    • Go live

      A title, a category, a thumbnail and you are on. Show your items, start auctions and reply to the chat in real time.

    • Create a listing from one photo

      AI fills in the title, category, condition and description, and suggests a price. Check it, publish it — live or as a direct sale.

    • Get paid on handover

      By post, take 2 photos (inside of the parcel and post office receipt) and enter the tracking number. You are paid as soon as the buyer confirms everything is fine, or 24 hours after delivery without a report, or 10 days after sending without news. In person, the buyer’s 4-digit code pays you straight away.

    Protected payment

    Your money is protected. So is the seller’s.

    Nobody pays into the void. When you buy, your payment is reserved. The seller is only paid once you have received the item and confirmed it, or once the time to report a problem has passed.

    1. You paysecurely, in the app
    2. Your payment is reservedthe seller doesn’t have it yet
    3. The item is handed overyou confirm receipt in the app
    4. The seller gets paidafter your OK, or 24 h without a report
    In-person handover

    Pickup: a 4-digit code

    If the seller allows pickup, you meet wherever suits you. You check the item on the spot, then give the 4-digit code shown in your app. Once the code is given, the sale is accepted and the seller is paid.

    • Check the item before giving the code
    • The code confirms you received the item
    • No shipping costs

    Never share your code remotely or before you have seen the item.

    Tracked shipping, you confirm receipt

    The seller sets the shipping cost (the app suggests a price) and you pay it at checkout. They send the parcel by tracked post, after taking 2 photos and entering the tracking number. When the parcel arrives, you confirm in the app:

    • “Everything is fine”: the seller is paid right away
    • A problem? You have 24 hours after receipt to report it, with photos
    • No news 10 days after shipping: the sale is accepted

    A problem with your order?

    Item never arrived or not as described: report it from your order within 24 hours of receipt, with photos, or write to support@shapop.app. The seller replies with their photos within 48 hours and our team decides. A return is only possible if the problem was reported in time.

    FAQ

    You might be wondering…

    Is it free?

    Yes, downloading ShaPop and buying are free. You only pay the item price and, if it is shipped, the shipping cost set by the seller.

    When does the seller get the money?

    As soon as the buyer confirms everything is fine. Otherwise 24 hours after receipt without a reported problem, or 10 days after sending without news. In person, as soon as the buyer gives their 4-digit code.

    How long do I have to report a problem?

    24 hours after receiving the parcel (or after its “delivered” date when the post office tracks it). Report it from your order, with photos. A return is only possible if you reported it within those 24 hours.

    Who handles delivery?

    The seller, by their own means. There is no built-in courier: in-person handover or shipping by post. After payment, you arrange everything directly via WhatsApp or email.

    Where is ShaPop available?

    On iPhone and Android, worldwide, in 5 languages: English, French, Spanish, Russian and Hebrew.

    What if the item never arrives?

    Report it from your order or write to support@shapop.app. We review the case and help you find a solution.
