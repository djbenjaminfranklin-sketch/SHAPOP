    For buyers

    Buy in 5 easy steps

    See the item on video, ask the seller your questions live, and only pay for what you have actually seen.

    1. Watch the live

      The seller shows the item from every angle and answers your questions in the chat. You can also browse listings outside of lives.

    2. Bid (or buy now)

      Bid in one tap. Set your maximum bid and ShaPop bids for you, never above your limit. Pre-bids, Buy now and offers work too.

    3. Pay in the app

      By card, Apple Pay, and Bit in Israel. ShaPop holds your money: the seller does not get it yet.

      Bank cardApple PayBit (Israel)

    4. Contact the seller

      Once you have paid, a WhatsApp button (or email) puts you in touch with the seller directly to arrange the meetup or the shipping.

    5. Get your item

      In person with your secret 4-digit code, or by post: shipping costs, set by the seller, are paid at checkout.

    For sellers

    Your stall, open anywhere, any time

    Clear out your wardrobe, run your small shop or sell your collectibles: ShaPop brings you live buyers and guaranteed payment.

    • Go live

      A title, a category, a thumbnail and you are on. Show your items, start auctions and reply to the chat in real time.

    • Create a listing from one photo

      AI fills in the title, category, condition and description, and suggests a price. Check it, publish it — live or as a direct sale.

    • Get paid on handover

      In person, enter the buyer’s 4-digit code. By post, send two photos: the inside of the parcel and the stamped shipping receipt. You’re paid straight away with the code, and 3 days after the photos (or as soon as the buyer confirms receipt) for a shipment.

    Two photos to get paid

    • A photo of the shipping receipt stamped by the post office
    • Shipping set by the seller, paid by the buyer at checkout

    FAQ

    You might be wondering…

    Is it free?

    Yes, downloading ShaPop and buying are free. You only pay the item price and, if it is shipped, the shipping cost set by the seller.

    When does the seller get the money?

    Only once the handover is proven: the 4-digit code entered in person, or the two shipping photos (inside of the parcel + stamped receipt). Until then, ShaPop holds the money. By post, the seller is paid 3 days after the photos, or as soon as the buyer confirms receipt.

    How does the 4-digit code work?

    After payment, your app shows a secret code. You give it to the seller when you receive the item; they enter it and the sale is complete. Only share it after checking the item.

    Who handles delivery?

    The seller, by their own means. There is no built-in courier: in-person handover or shipping by post. After payment, you arrange everything directly via WhatsApp or email.

    Where is ShaPop available?

    ShaPop is available on iPhone worldwide, in 5 languages: English, French, Spanish, Russian and Hebrew.

    What if the item never arrives?

    Report it from your order or write to support@shapop.app. We review the case and help you find a solution.
