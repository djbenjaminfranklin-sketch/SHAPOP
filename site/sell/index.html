    Why live

    Why sell live on ShaPop?

    Auctions push prices up

    When several buyers want the same item, they bid against each other in real time. You set the starting price, the room does the rest.

    Buyers trust what they see

    You show the item from every angle and answer questions in the chat. Fewer doubts, fewer returns.

    AI does the boring part

    Take your photos: the AI names the item, picks the category, writes the description and suggests a price.

    A guaranteed payment

    The amount is reserved on the buyer’s card as soon as they win. No unpaid orders, no chasing anyone.

    How it works

    From your wardrobe to your first live in 4 steps

    1. Create your account

      Here, in one minute. Then sign in to the ShaPop app with the same email and finish your seller profile.

    2. Prepare your items

      Snap your photos: the AI names them, sorts them and suggests a price. You check and adjust.

    3. Go live and sell at auction

      A title, a thumbnail, and you are on air. Start each auction, chat with buyers, sell several items in a row.

    4. Get paid when they receive it

      You ship (or hand over in person). As soon as the buyer confirms — or 24 hours after delivery without any problem — the money is yours.

    Fees

    Simple fees, only when you sell

    8% commission

    On the sale price, plus VAT where applicable. Nothing if you don’t sell.

    Card payment fees

    Charged by the payment provider (Stripe): 2.9% + $0.30 per sale, plus VAT where applicable.

    No hidden costs

    No subscription, no listing fee, no fee to go live. Shipping is paid by the buyer.

    Protection

    Protected on both sides

    • Payment reserved on the card

      When the auction ends, the amount is reserved on the buyer’s card. It cannot disappear.

    • 24 hours to report, not more

      The buyer has 24 hours after delivery to report a problem, with photos. After that, the sale is final.

    • Clear rules for everyone

      Your shipping photos protect you in case of dispute. Our team decides based on the evidence.

    Questions from sellers

    Do I need a company to sell?

    No. Individuals can sell their own items. If you sell regularly as a business, you must follow the rules that apply in your country.

    When do I get paid?

    As soon as the buyer confirms everything is fine, or 24 hours after delivery without a report, or 10 days after shipping without news. In person: as soon as the buyer gives you their 4-digit code.

    What can I sell?

    Fashion, sneakers, bags, watches, cards, toys, tech, home… Anything legal that you own. Counterfeits, dangerous or illegal items are forbidden.

    Do I have to go live?

    No. You can also list items as direct sales, which stay on sale between your lives.

    Is it the same account as in the app?

    Yes. The email and password you create here are the ones you use in the ShaPop app.
