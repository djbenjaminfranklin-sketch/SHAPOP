    Buying and selling rules

    These rules protect both the buyer and the seller. They apply to every order.

    If you buy

    1. You pay at the end of the auction or when you tap “Buy now”. The payment is collected at the time of purchase, on behalf of the seller. You choose pickup or delivery by post.
    2. As soon as you receive your parcel, confirm it in the app. “Everything is fine” pays the seller right away.
    3. If something is wrong, you have 24 hours after receipt to report it, with photos. When the post office tracks the parcel, the 24 hours start from its “delivered” date, even if you have not confirmed.
    4. After those 24 hours without a report, the seller is paid.
    5. Without news 10 days after the parcel was sent, the seller is paid.
    6. The 24 hours are only for ShaPop protection. They do not reduce your legal rights in any way (14-day withdrawal with a professional seller, conformity guarantee, hidden defects).
    7. After 2 disputes judged unfounded, you can no longer use ShaPop protection. This never affects your legal rights, and a person reviews it on request.
    8. Live giveaways are free, with no purchase needed, for people aged 18 and over with an account. The seller asks a question: the first right answer wins (3 tries per gift). Apple and Google are not organisers or partners.

    If you sell

    1. Say whether you sell as a private person or as a professional. A “Private” or “Pro” badge is shown on each of your lives. A professional gives their trade name, registration number and address, shown to buyers before payment. Saying you are private when you sell as a business leads to suspension.
    2. Send the item within the time you chose. Otherwise the order is cancelled and the buyer refunded.
    3. Send by tracked post. Before confirming the shipment, take the 2 photos (inside of the parcel with the item, and the post office receipt) and type the tracking number. The photos protect you if there is a dispute.
    4. You are paid 24 hours after the buyer confirms receipt (or right away if they say everything is fine), or 10 days after sending without news from the buyer; 30 days for luxury items.
    5. If the buyer reports a problem, the money stays on hold. Reply with your photos within 48 hours; our team decides.
    6. 1st dispute lost: warning. 2nd: for 30 days, you are only paid once the buyer has received the item. 3rd: your account is closed.
    7. As a professional, your buyers can cancel within 14 days after receipt, without a reason. As a private seller, you choose your return policy (it can be “no returns”).

    Pickup in person

    1. The buyer checks the item on the spot, then gives their 4-digit code. Once the code is given, the handover is confirmed.

    Luxury items (500 € or more, or a major brand)

    1. An item is a luxury item if it costs €500 or more (or the equivalent in your currency, for example ₪2,000) or if it is from a major luxury brand (Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Dior, Rolex, Cartier…). The seller can also tick “luxury brand item” themselves. The €500 threshold applies to the price of one single item, never to the order total: several cheap items bought together are not a luxury item.
    2. To list it, the seller must tick: “I certify that this item is authentic. I know that selling a counterfeit is a crime and that if it is fake I will have to refund the buyer, pay a penalty of 50% of the price, and that my account will be closed.” Without this box, the listing is refused.
    3. The seller is paid 30 days after receipt, even if the buyer says “Everything is fine”. Without confirmed receipt, the 10 days without news become 30 days.
    4. The buyer has 30 days after receipt to report a counterfeit, with proof (photos, expert opinion, the brand’s opinion). The listing is then removed and the seller’s payout for this sale is frozen. The 24 hours still apply to other problems.
    5. The seller has 7 days to answer in the app: admit it, or contest it with proof (invoice, certificate, photos). If they contest, a person from ShaPop looks at the evidence of both sides. If they admit it or do not answer: the buyer is fully refunded, shipping included; the seller owes a penalty of 50% of the sale price (at most the sale price), withheld from what ShaPop owes them; their account is closed for good. The seller can always contest by email at support@shapop.app. The buyer can file a police complaint.
