    Buying and selling rules

    These rules protect both the buyer and the seller. They apply to every order.

    If you buy

    1. At the end of the auction, the amount is reserved on your card. You choose pickup or delivery by post.
    2. As soon as you receive your parcel, confirm it in the app. “Everything is fine” pays the seller right away.
    3. If something is wrong, you have 24 hours after receipt to report it, with photos. When the post office tracks the parcel, the 24 hours start from its “delivered” date, even if you have not confirmed.
    4. After those 24 hours without a report, the sale is accepted and the seller is paid.
    5. Without news 10 days after the parcel was sent, the sale is accepted and the seller is paid.
    6. A return is only possible if you reported a problem within the 24 hours.
    7. After 2 disputes judged unfounded, you can no longer open disputes or request returns.

    If you sell

    1. Send the item within the time you chose. Otherwise the order is cancelled and the buyer refunded.
    2. Send by tracked post. Before confirming the shipment, take the 2 photos (inside of the parcel with the item, and the post office receipt) and type the tracking number. The photos protect you if there is a dispute.
    3. You are paid as soon as the buyer says everything is fine, or 24 hours after receipt without a problem, or 10 days after sending without news.
    4. If the buyer reports a problem, the money stays blocked. Reply with your photos within 48 hours; our team decides.
    5. 1st dispute lost: warning. 2nd: for 30 days, you are only paid once the buyer has received the item. 3rd: your account is closed.

    Pickup in person

    1. The buyer checks the item on the spot, then gives their 4-digit code. Once the code is given, the sale is accepted.
