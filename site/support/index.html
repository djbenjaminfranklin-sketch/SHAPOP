    Frequently asked questions

    Buying & paying

    Which payment methods are accepted?

    Bank card or Apple Pay. Payment always happens in the app: never pay a seller outside ShaPop, you would lose the protection.

    How does the automatic max bid work?

    You enter the maximum amount you are willing to pay. If someone bids, ShaPop automatically outbids them for you, step by step, never going above your maximum.

    Is a bid binding?

    Yes. If you win the auction, you commit to buying the item. If you made a mistake, write to us straight away.

    Who pays for shipping?

    The buyer, at checkout. The amount is set by the seller (the app suggests a price) and shown before you pay.

    Getting your item

    How do I contact the seller?

    After payment, a WhatsApp button and the seller’s email address appear in your order. You arrange things directly.

    I gave my code too early. What now?

    The code confirms you received the item, so the seller is then paid. If you gave it before checking the item and there is a problem, write to us immediately at support@shapop.app.

    The seller is not answering.

    Follow up by WhatsApp or email. If they still don’t answer, contact us: as long as the handover isn’t proven, they don’t get paid.

    What proof does the seller need for a shipment?

    A photo of the inside of the parcel with the item in it, and a photo of the shipping receipt stamped by the post office. Keep the tracking number if there is one.

    Selling

    How do I get paid?

    By post: send by tracked post, take the 2 photos (inside of the parcel and post office receipt) and enter the tracking number. You are paid as soon as the buyer confirms everything is fine, or 24 hours after delivery without a report, or 10 days after sending without news. In person: the buyer’s 4-digit code pays you straight away. The ShaPop commission is deducted.

    How do I set my shipping cost?

    When you create your listing, you enter your shipping cost; the app suggests a price based on the type of item. The buyer pays it together with the item.

    What am I not allowed to sell?

    Anything illegal, dangerous, counterfeit or stolen, as well as digital goods. The full list is in the terms of use.

    Account & safety

    How do I delete my account?

    From the app settings. Deletion is permanent; some transaction data is kept for as long as the law requires.

    How do I report a user or a live?

    Use the report button in the app, or write to us with the account name and a screenshot.
