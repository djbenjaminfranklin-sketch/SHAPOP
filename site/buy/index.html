    How it works

    Buying live in 5 steps

    1. Follow sellers

      Pick your categories and follow the sellers you like: you are notified when they go live.

    2. Bid in one tap

      Watch the live, ask your questions in the chat and bid. You can also set a maximum and let the app bid for you.

    3. Your card is reserved only if you win

      Lost the auction? Nothing happens. Won it? The amount is reserved on your card, not paid to the seller yet.

    4. Hand-delivered or shipped

      Choose to meet the seller in person or to have the item sent by post.

    5. 24 hours to check

      Once you have the item, confirm it’s fine or report a problem within 24 hours, with photos.

    Protection

    Your money is protected

    • The seller is paid after you receive it

      The money only reaches the seller once you have the item, or after the 24-hour check period.

    • A problem? Report it

      Item not as described or never arrived: report it from your order. Our team reviews the photos and decides.

    • Always pay in the app

      Never pay a seller outside ShaPop: you would lose the protection.
