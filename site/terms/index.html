    1. The service

    ShaPop is a marketplace where private people and professional sellers sell physical items in live video: auctions, offers and “Buy now” during a live. ShaPop acts as an intermediary: it is neither the seller nor the buyer, and the sale contract is concluded directly between them. ShaPop provides the app, collects the payment on behalf of the seller and checks the handover.

    By creating an account or using the app, you accept these terms and our privacy policy.

    2. Your account

    • You can watch lives from age 13. To buy, sell or take part in giveaways, you must be 18 or over and able to enter into a contract.
    • Your information must be accurate and up to date.
    • Your account is personal: you are responsible for what happens on it and for keeping your login details secret.

    3. Private or professional seller

    • Every seller says whether they sell as a private person or as a professional. A “Private” or “Pro” badge is shown on each of their lives.
    • A professional gives their trade name, registration number and address. Buyers see them before paying, by tapping the badge. A professional also follows the rules of their country (invoices, taxes, consumer law).
    • Saying you are a private person when you really sell as a business is forbidden. It leads to the suspension of your account.

    4. Rules for sellers

    • You only sell physical, legal items that belong to you.
    • Your descriptions, photos and videos are honest and show the real item, flaws included.
    • AI-written listings are suggestions: you must check them and you remain responsible for them.
    • You honour every completed sale and hand over the item within the time you chose.

    5. Lives and content

    You keep ownership of your content, but you give ShaPop a worldwide, non-exclusive, free licence to host, stream and display it as part of the service. Forbidden: illegal, hateful, violent, sexual or misleading content, harassment, and infringement of others’ rights. ShaPop may moderate, end a live, remove a listing or suspend an account. When a live is recorded, a message on screen says so.

    6. Auctions, offers and Buy now

    • All sales happen during a live.
    • A bid is a firm commitment: if you win, you must buy the item.
    • With the automatic max bid, ShaPop bids for you in steps, up to the ceiling you set and never above it.
    • Pre-bids placed before a live count as bids.
    • “Buy now” and accepting an offer complete the sale.
    • Bidding on your own items or pushing prices up artificially is forbidden.
    • These live auctions are not public auctions within the meaning of consumer law: your legal rights (including the 14-day withdrawal right with a professional seller) still apply.

    7. Live giveaways

    Sellers can offer gifts during their lives. These giveaways are free contests:

    • Organiser: ShaPop (Benjamin Taieb EI). The prize is given by the seller, who hands it to the winner like an order (in person or by post).
    • Free: no purchase needed. Open to people aged 18 and over with a ShaPop account.
    • How it works: the seller asks a question during the live. The first person to give the right answer wins. Speed and knowledge decide, not luck. Each person has 3 tries per gift. There is one winner per gift. If nobody finds the answer before the end of the live, the gift is cancelled.
    • The winner is announced in the live. The gift cannot be exchanged for money.

    Apple and Google are not organisers or partners of these giveaways and are not involved in them in any way.

    8. Prices, payment and fees

    • Prices are shown in the currency displayed in the app; the total amount (item + any shipping) is shown before payment.
    • Payment is made only in the app (bank card or Apple Pay). A payment made outside ShaPop is not protected.
    • The seller sets their own shipping cost (the app suggests a price); the buyer pays it at checkout.
    • Signing up and buying are free.
    • ShaPop commission: 8% of the sale price, plus payment fees of 2.9% + €0.30. VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French General Tax Code (CGI). These amounts are taken from the money paid to the seller.

    9. Collection on behalf of the seller and protected payment

    ShaPop acts as an intermediary, in the name and on behalf of the seller. By signing up, the seller gives ShaPop a mandate to collect the price of their sales in their name and on their behalf, to deduct the commission and the fees, then to pay them the balance. The sale contract is concluded directly between the buyer and the seller. Card payments are processed by Stripe; payouts to sellers go through Stripe or PayPal. The money collected for each seller is tracked separately, sale by sale.

    The payment is collected at the time of purchase. The seller is paid 24 hours after the buyer confirms receipt (or right away if the buyer confirms that everything is fine), or 10 days after shipping without news from the buyer; 30 days for luxury items. If the handover does not happen or in case of a dispute, ShaPop keeps the money on hold or refunds the buyer (see “Disputes”).

    10. Handing over the item

    ShaPop has no built-in courier. After payment, the buyer and the seller are put in touch (WhatsApp or email) to arrange things. There are two ways to hand over an item, chosen by the seller:

    • In person (if the seller allows it): the buyer has a 4-digit code in the app. They only share it at the moment of handover, after checking the item. When the seller enters the code, this confirms that the item was received as described.
    • By post: the seller packs the item carefully and sends it by tracked post. In the app, they add a photo of the inside of the parcel showing the item, a photo of the post office receipt, and the tracking number. This proof must be genuine and recent. On receipt, the buyer confirms it in the app.

    The buyer then has 24 hours to report a problem to ShaPop (item not received, or not as described) before the money is paid to the seller. This period is only for ShaPop protection. It does not reduce your legal rights in any way (14-day withdrawal with a professional seller, conformity guarantee, hidden defects).

    For an in-person handover, choose a busy public place. Any false proof leads to account suspension and repayment of the amounts.

    11. Disputes

    If the item is not received or does not match the listing, the buyer reports it from their order or at support@shapop.app. ShaPop reviews the evidence (handover photos, messages, postal tracking, recording of the live if there is one) and may ask both parties for information. Depending on the case, ShaPop may refund the buyer, pay the seller or suggest a return.

    • A seller who loses disputes gets a warning, then is only paid after receipt for 30 days, then their account is closed.
    • After 2 disputes judged unfounded, a buyer can no longer use ShaPop protection.

    These rules only concern ShaPop protection and the money held by ShaPop. They never take away your legal rights. A person reviews any decision on request at support@shapop.app.

    12. Luxury items and counterfeits

    A luxury item is any item sold for €500 or more (or the equivalent in the seller’s currency) or from a major luxury brand (for example Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Dior, Rolex or Cartier). The seller can also state that the item is from a luxury brand. The €500 threshold applies to the price of one single item, never to the order total: several cheap items bought together are not a luxury item.

    • Certification: to list a luxury item, the seller must tick “I certify that this item is authentic. I know that selling a counterfeit is a crime and that if it is fake I will have to refund the buyer, pay a penalty of 50% of the price, and that my account will be closed.” The date and the seller’s identity are recorded. Without this certification, the listing is refused.
    • Seller payout: for a luxury item, the seller is paid 30 days after receipt, even if the buyer confirms “Everything is fine”. Without confirmed receipt, the 10-day period becomes 30 days.
    • Reporting a fake: the buyer has 30 days after receipt to report a counterfeit, with proof (photos, expert opinion, the brand’s opinion). The 24-hour period still applies to other problems.
    • What happens next: the listing is removed right away and the seller’s payout for this sale is frozen. The seller is told why and has 7 days to answer in the app: admit it, or contest it with proof (invoice, certificate, photos).
    • If the seller contests: a person from ShaPop examines the evidence of both sides before any penalty.
    • Fake upheld: if the seller admits it or does not answer within 7 days, the counterfeit is upheld. The buyer is fully refunded, shipping included. The seller owes a penalty of 50% of the sale price, capped at the sale price, withheld from the amounts ShaPop owes the seller. The seller’s account is closed permanently and the evidence is kept. The buyer may file a complaint with the police.
    • The seller can always contest a decision by email at support@shapop.app.

    13. Withdrawal, returns and legal guarantees

    With a professional seller (“Pro” badge):

    • You can cancel your purchase within 14 days after receiving the item, without giving a reason. This also applies to items bought at auction in a live, which are not public auctions.
    • The seller refunds you within 14 days. Return costs are paid by you, unless the seller agrees to pay them.
    • In Israel, the seller may keep at most 5% of the price or ₪100, whichever is lower. Some people (aged 65 and over, people with disabilities, new immigrants) have 4 months in the cases set by law.
    • You also keep the legal conformity guarantee (2 years in the European Union).

    With a private seller (“Private” badge): there is no legal right of withdrawal and no legal conformity guarantee, but the seller remains liable for hidden defects. The seller chooses their own return policy, which can be “no returns”.

    In every case, ShaPop protection covers items not received or not as described (see “Handing over the item” and “Disputes”).

You must also respect the import and export rules of the countries involved.
