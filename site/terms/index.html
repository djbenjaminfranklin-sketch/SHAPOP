    1. The service

    ShaPop is a marketplace that lets individuals and small sellers sell physical items through live video, auctions or direct sales. ShaPop is neither the seller nor the buyer: the sale contract is concluded directly between them. ShaPop provides the platform, the protected payment and the handover check.

    By creating an account or using the app, you accept these terms and our privacy policy.

    2. Your account

    • You must be at least 18 and able to enter into a contract.
    • Your information must be accurate and up to date.
    • Your account is personal: you are responsible for what happens on it and for keeping your login details confidential.
    • A seller acting as a business must say so and comply with the obligations of their country (invoicing, taxes, consumer law).

    3. Rules for sellers

    • You only sell physical, legal items that belong to you.
    • Your descriptions, photos and videos are honest and show the real item, flaws included.
    • AI-generated listings are suggestions: you must check them and remain responsible for them.
    • You honour every completed sale and hand over the item within a reasonable time.

    4. Lives and content

    You keep ownership of your content, but you grant ShaPop a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, stream and display it as part of the service. Forbidden: illegal, hateful, violent, sexual or misleading content, harassment, and infringement of others’ rights. ShaPop may moderate, end a live, remove a listing or suspend an account.

    5. Auctions, offers and Buy now

    • A bid is a firm commitment: if you win, you must buy the item.
    • With the automatic max bid, ShaPop bids for you in steps, up to the ceiling you set and never above it.
    • Pre-bids placed before a live have the same value as a bid.
    • Buy now and accepting an offer complete the sale.
    • Bidding on your own items or artificially inflating prices is forbidden.

    6. Prices, payment and fees

    • Prices are shown in the currency displayed in the app; the total amount (item + any shipping) is shown before payment.
    • Payment is made only in the app (bank card or Apple Pay), through licensed payment providers. A payment made outside ShaPop is not protected.
    • The seller sets their own shipping cost (the app suggests a price); the buyer pays it at checkout.
    • Signing up and buying are free. ShaPop charges a commission on each sale, deducted from the amount paid to the seller; its rate is shown in the app before listing.

    7. Protected payment

    When the buyer pays, ShaPop holds the amount. It is only paid to the seller after proof of handover (section 8). If the handover does not happen or in case of a dispute, ShaPop may keep the payment on hold or refund the buyer under section 9.

    8. Handing over the item

    ShaPop has no built-in courier. After payment, the buyer and the seller are put in touch (WhatsApp or email) to arrange things. There are two ways to hand over an item, chosen by the seller:

    • In person (if the seller allows it): the buyer has a 4-digit code in the app. They only share it at the moment of handover, after checking the item. When the seller enters the code, this confirms the item was received as described and triggers the payout.
    • By post: the seller packs the item carefully and sends it by tracked post. In the app, they add a photo of the inside of the parcel showing the item, a photo of the shipping receipt stamped by the post office, and the tracking number. This evidence must be genuine and recent. On receipt, the buyer confirms it in the app: if they choose “Everything is fine”, the seller is paid right away; otherwise the buyer has 24 hours after receipt to report a problem, after which the seller is paid. With no news from the buyer, the seller is paid 10 days after shipping. Luxury items follow section 10.

    For an in-person handover, choose a busy public place. Any false proof leads to account suspension and repayment of the amounts.

    9. Disputes

    If the item is not received or does not match the listing, the buyer reports it as soon as possible from their order or at support@shapop.app. ShaPop reviews the available evidence (handover photos, messages, postal tracking) and may ask both parties for information. Depending on the case, ShaPop may refund the buyer, pay the seller or suggest a return. These decisions only concern the funds held by ShaPop and do not take away anyone’s legal rights.

    10. Luxury items and counterfeits

    A luxury item is any item sold for 500 € or more (or the equivalent in the seller’s currency) or from a major luxury brand (for example Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Dior, Rolex or Cartier). The seller can also state that the item is from a luxury brand. The 500 € threshold applies to the price of one single item, never to the order total: several cheap items bought together are not a luxury item.

    • Certification: to list a luxury item, the seller must tick “I certify that this item is authentic. I know that selling a counterfeit is a crime and that if it is fake I will have to refund the buyer, pay a penalty of 50% of the price, and that my account will be closed.” The date and the seller’s identity are recorded. Without this certification, the listing is refused.
    • Seller payout: for a luxury item, the seller is paid 30 days after receipt, even if the buyer confirms “Everything is fine”. Without confirmed receipt, the 10-day period becomes 30 days.
    • Reporting a fake: the buyer has 30 days after receipt to report a counterfeit, with proof (photos, expert opinion, the brand’s opinion). The 24-hour period still applies to other problems.
    • Confirmed fake: if the ShaPop team confirms the counterfeit, the buyer is fully refunded, shipping included; a penalty of 50% of the price is taken from the seller’s next payouts; the seller’s account is closed permanently; the evidence is kept. The buyer may file a complaint with the police.

    11. Cancellation and consumer rights

    Between individuals, a completed sale can only be cancelled with both parties’ agreement or through a dispute. When the seller is a business, the buyer also has the rights granted by the consumer protection law of their country (for example a right of withdrawal for distance sales).

You must also respect the import and export rules of the countries involved.
