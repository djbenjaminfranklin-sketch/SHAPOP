    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 23 November 2026

    This policy explains what data ShaPop collects when you use the ShaPop app and the shapop.app website, why we use it and what your rights are. We apply the data protection laws of the countries where the service is offered, including the GDPR for users in the European Union and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law.

    Who we are

    The ShaPop service is operated by ShaPop (“ShaPop”, “we”), the controller of your personal data. For any question: support@shapop.app.

    1. The data we collect

    • Account: name, email address, phone number, profile photo, country, login details (including “Sign in with Apple”).
    • Content you publish: video and audio of your lives, listings, item photos, chat messages, comments and reviews.
    • Transactions: purchases, sales, bids, offers, amounts, shipping costs, handover status. Payments are processed by licensed payment providers: we never see or store your full card number or your bank or Bit credentials.
    • Proof of handover: validation of the 4-digit code, photos of the inside of the parcel and of the stamped shipping receipt sent by the seller, with their date.
    • Approximate location (city) for communities and the map of lives, only if you allow it.
    • Technical data: device model, app version, language, crash logs, notification token.

    2. Why we use it

    • to run the service: lives, chat, auctions, purchases, notifications;
    • to collect payments, hold them, then pay the seller once the handover is proven;
    • to put the buyer and seller in touch after a payment;
    • to handle disputes and answer support requests;
    • to keep the platform safe, prevent fraud and enforce our terms;
    • to personalise the lives we suggest to you;
    • to meet our legal obligations (accounting, anti-money-laundering, requests from authorities).

    Depending on the case, this processing is based on performing our contract with you, our legal obligations, our legitimate interest (safety, improving the service) or your consent (location, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time.

    3. Putting you in touch after a purchase

    Only after a payment do we let the buyer and the seller contact each other (WhatsApp button with the phone number, or email address) to arrange the handover. This sharing is limited to the order concerned. Conversations on WhatsApp or by email are subject to those services’ terms; use these contact details only for the order.

    4. Who we share it with

    • Other users: your public profile, lives, listings and reviews are visible.
    • Payment providers, to process and secure payments and payouts.
    • Our technical providers: hosting, video streaming, email and notification delivery, crash analysis, and the AI service that analyses an item photo to write the listing.
    • Authorities, when the law requires it.

    We never sell your personal data.

    5. International transfers

    ShaPop is used in many countries and some of our providers are located outside your country. When your data is transferred, we put in place the safeguards required by applicable law (for example the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses).

    6. How long we keep it

    • Account: as long as it is active, then deleted at your request.
    • Transactions and proof of handover: as long as needed to handle disputes and meet our accounting and legal obligations.
    • Lives: videos are streamed in real time; if a recording is kept (moderation, dispute), it is deleted after a limited period.
    • Support messages: as long as needed to handle your request, then archived for a limited time.

    7. Security

    Encrypted connections, restricted access to data, certified payment providers: we take appropriate technical and organisational measures. No system is infallible; let us know if you notice suspicious activity on your account.

    8. Your rights

    Depending on the law of your country (including the GDPR in the European Union and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law), you can access, correct and delete your data, restrict or object to certain processing, request portability and withdraw your consent. You can delete your account at any time from the app settings. To exercise your rights: support@shapop.app. You can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

    9. Minors

    ShaPop is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about minors; if this happens, write to us and we will delete it.

    10. Website and cookies

    The shapop.app website uses no advertising cookies or trackers. It only stores, in your browser, the language you have chosen.

    11. Changes

    We may update this policy. If there is a significant change, we will let you know in the app or by email. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
