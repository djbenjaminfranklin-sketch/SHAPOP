    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 28 November 2026

    This policy explains what data ShaPop collects when you use the ShaPop app and the shapop.app website, why we use it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. We apply the GDPR (European Union) and, for users in Israel, the Israeli Privacy Protection Law.

    1. The data we collect

    Required data (without it, the matching feature cannot work):

    • To create an account: email address and display name, plus your login details (including “Sign in with Apple”).
    • To buy: the address where the item is handed over or delivered.
    • To sell: whether you sell as a private person or a professional, and your payout method (Stripe or PayPal). A professional also gives their trade name, registration number and business address.

    Optional data: profile photo, phone number (for the WhatsApp button after a sale), city, preferences (categories, sellers you follow). You can leave these out.

    Data created when you use ShaPop:

    • Content you publish: video and audio of your lives, item photos, chat messages, comments and reviews.
    • Transactions: purchases, sales, bids, offers, giveaway answers, amounts, shipping costs, handover status. Card payments are processed by Stripe: we never see or store your full card number or your bank details.
    • Proof of handover: the 4-digit code check, photos of the inside of the parcel and of the post office receipt sent by the seller, the tracking number, with their dates.
    • Approximate location (city) for communities and the map of lives, only if you allow it.
    • Technical data: device model, app version, language, crash logs, notification token.

    2. Why we use it

    • to run the service: lives, chat, auctions, giveaways, purchases, notifications;
    • to collect payments on behalf of sellers, hold them until the handover, then pay the seller;
    • to put the buyer and seller in touch after a payment;
    • to handle disputes and answer support requests;
    • to keep the platform safe, prevent fraud and enforce our terms;
    • to suggest lives that may interest you;
    • to meet our legal obligations (accounting, anti-money-laundering, requests from authorities).

    Depending on the case, this processing is based on our contract with you, our legal obligations, our legitimate interest (safety, preventing fraud, improving the service) or your consent (location, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time.

    3. Putting you in touch after a purchase

    Only after a payment do we let the buyer and the seller contact each other (WhatsApp button with the phone number, or email address) to arrange the handover. This sharing is limited to the order concerned. Conversations on WhatsApp or by email are subject to those services’ terms; use these contact details only for the order.

    4. Recording of lives

    Some lives may be recorded. When a live is recorded, a message on screen tells the seller and the viewers. We record lives only to have proof if there is a dispute and for safety (for example to check a reported fraud). The recording is not published.

    5. Automatic decisions

    • Contact details in the chat: before a payment, the app automatically spots messages that share a phone number, an email or a link to pay outside ShaPop, and blocks them. This protects you from scams.
    • Sanctions after disputes: the app counts the disputes lost by a seller (warning, then payment only after receipt for 30 days, then account closed) and the disputes judged unfounded for a buyer (ShaPop protection no longer available). It applies these steps automatically.

    You can always ask for a person to review one of these decisions: write to support@shapop.app.

    6. Who we share it with

    • Other users: your public profile, lives, listings, reviews and your “Private” or “Pro” badge are visible. A professional’s trade name, registration number and address are shown to buyers.
    • Our providers, only for what they do for us:
      • Supabase (database, European Union – Sweden);
      • Render (server, Germany);
      • Vercel (website, United States and European Union);
      • LiveKit (live video, United States);
      • Stripe (payments, Ireland and United States);
      • PayPal (payouts to sellers, Luxembourg);
      • Resend (emails, United States);
      • Ship24 (parcel tracking);
      • Anthropic (AI help to write listings from a photo, United States);
      • Apple and Google (notifications).
    • Authorities, when the law requires it.

    We never sell your personal data.

    7. International transfers

    Some of our providers are outside the European Union. When your data leaves the EU, it is protected by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For users in Israel, transfers abroad follow the Israeli Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations of 2001.

    8. How long we keep it

    • Account: as long as it exists.
    • After you delete your account: order and dispute data are kept for 3 years in an archive; accounting records for 10 years.
    • Chat messages in lives: 1 year.
    • Recording of a live (if recording is on): 30 days, or until the end of a dispute.
    • Technical logs: 90 days.

    9. Security

    Encrypted connections, restricted access to data, certified payment providers: we take appropriate technical and organisational measures. No system is perfect; let us know if you notice suspicious activity on your account.

    10. Your rights

    You can access your data, correct it, delete it, object to some uses, ask to limit them, get a copy to take elsewhere (portability) and withdraw your consent. You can delete your account at any time from the app settings. To use your rights: support@shapop.app.

    You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or, if you live in Israel, to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

    11. Minors

    You can watch lives from age 13. To buy, sell or take part in giveaways, you must be 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 13; if this happens, write to us and we will delete it.

    12. Website and cookies

    The shapop.app website uses no advertising cookies or trackers. It only stores, in your browser, the language you have chosen.

    13. Changes

    We may update this policy. If there is a significant change, we will let you know in the app or by email. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
